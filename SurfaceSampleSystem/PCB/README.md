Surface Sampling System(SSS) is one of the subsystems for science payload.
SSS controls four servo motors to scoop the dirt.
When the scoop is closed, there is a holding torque(~50mA).
When the scoop is open, the servo shouldn't consume any power.
Only one scoop will be open at a time, and each servo should have current feedback to know if the holding torque is too much or not.
This custom PCB has been reviewed by electrical lead of robotics club.
